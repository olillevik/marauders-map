# Marauders Map

The vocabulary of the Marauders Map app, which shows vessels off the coast of Norway using AIS data.

## Language

**Vessel**:
A ship that broadcasts AIS position reports, identified by its MMSI.
_Avoid_: Boat

**MMSI**:
The Maritime Mobile Service Identity, the identifier that each AIS position report carries for its vessel.

**AIS**:
The Automatic Identification System, through which vessels broadcast their position reports.

**Position**:
The most recent position a vessel has reported, as the app last received it. It may be up to 10 minutes behind the newest report. No age limit applies yet, so an old position still counts as the vessel's position.
